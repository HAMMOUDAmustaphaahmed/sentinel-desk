---
title: Why was my card payment declined?
keywords: card payment declined not working refused
---

Hello, a card payment is most often declined for one of these reasons: the card is not activated or is frozen, the payment is above your limit, or the merchant asked for a currency or payment type your card settings block. You can check all three in the app under Cards. If everything looks right there, reply with the date, amount and merchant and a support engineer will look into it.
